Assignment: Interactive Simulator

Task:
Design an interactive simulator where you can apply forces to a ball using your keyboard.

Rules:

Assume the ball is passed to you by a teammate.

You must kick the ball from a position at least 1 meter away from the goal.

Use keyboard keys (e.g., W/A/S/D) to apply forces in different directions.

Bonus Points:

Submit both your code and a short video of yourself playing the game (showing your hand on the keyboard and your laptop screen).

Extra credit will be given for successfully scoring a goal in the video.
